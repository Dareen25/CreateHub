# CreateHub – Creative Local Marketplace

## Description

**CreateHub** is a creative multi-category e-commerce marketplace designed for people who love art, DIY projects, handmade products, and creativity.

The app provides a complete shopping experience where users can discover creative products, purchase art supplies and DIY tools, explore artworks created by local artists, and find unique handmade gifts.

The marketplace is divided into three main sections:

🎨 **Artists & Art**

* Art supplies and tools
* Brushes, paints, pencils, canvases, and drawing materials
* Artists' Gallery for displaying and discovering artworks
* Paintings, digital art, sketches, and creative works

🛠️ **DIY & Makers**

* Hand tools
* DIY materials
* Wood and crafting materials
* Electronics and maker kits
* Arduino and beginner project kits

🎁 **Handmade & Gifts**

* Handmade products
* Customized gifts
* Decorative items
* Handmade accessories
* Gift boxes
* Home decoration products
